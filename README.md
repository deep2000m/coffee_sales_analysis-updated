**☕ Coffee Sales Analysis — Profitability vs Demand**

**📌 Overview**
This project analyzes coffee sales data to identify why strong sales ($45K+) are not translating into profit (~2.8% margin).
The focus is on understanding product preferences, regional behavior, seasonality, and customer retention to uncover performance gaps.

**🔍 Key Insights**
- 
**💰 Profitability Gap**
1. High sales volume but very low margin (~2.8%)
2. Indicates issues in pricing, cost structure, or discounting

**🌍 Regional Insights**
1. United States: Highest sales, customers, and AOV (work-driven consumption)
2. Ireland: Lower demand, more lifestyle-oriented
3. United Kingdom: No sales in May (potential anomaly)

**🫘 Product Insights**
1. Top Products: Liberca > Excelsa > Arabica
- Niche Product: Robusta
- Preferred Roast: Light Roast
**Top Sellers:**
- Arabica (Medium)
- Excelsa (Light)
- Robusta (Light)
**📦 Packaging Behavior**
- 2.5kg pack dominates sales
- Liberca leads (~35%), followed by Excelsa
→ Indicates strong bulk/value preference

**📅 Seasonal Patterns**
- Excelsa: Declines after August → likely summer preference
- Liberca: Increases in colder months → winter preference
- Arabica: Stable across year → all-season product
- Robusta: Peaks Feb–April
→ Sales are season-driven, not time-trend driven (corr ≈ -0.02)

**🎯 Loyalty Program Analysis**
- Profit contribution:
- Loyalty → 47%
- Non-loyalty → 52%
- Repeat customers: 21%
- Non-loyalty dominates 1kg purchases (~35%)
→ Loyalty program has minimal impact on retention or revenue

**🧠 Key Takeaways**
1. Demand is strong and stable
2. Product preferences are well-defined
3. Core issues:
- Low profitability
- Ineffective loyalty program
- Lack of seasonal and product optimization

**🚀 Recommendations**
1. Introduce Larger Packs (5kg)
- Target bulk buyers (cafés, offices) and scale high-volume demand.
2. Seasonal Product Strategy
- Promote Excelsa in summer
- Promote Liberca in winter
- Push Robusta early in the year
3. Revamp Loyalty Program
- Tier-based rewards
- Discounts & bundles
- Subscription model
4. Region-Specific Strategy
- US: Subscription + bulk focus
- Ireland: Premium/lifestyle positioning
- UK: Investigate missing sales period
5. Improve Margins
- Optimize pricing and sourcing
- Reduce excessive discounting
- Introduce premium offerings
6. Packaging Optimization
- Expand larger packs
- Improve 1kg targeting for new customers
  
**🛠️ Tools Used**
- Power BI → Dashboard & visualization
- Excel → Data cleaning & analysis
- DAX → Metrics & calculations

**✨ Conclusion**
- 
The business does not have a demand problem—it has a profitability and optimization problem.
By aligning product strategy, pricing, and customer retention efforts, significant improvements can be achieved.
